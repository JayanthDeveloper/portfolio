💡 Best Practices for Logistics Professionals:

  • Use unique passwords: Never reuse across TMS, WMS, ERP, email
  • Enable MFA: Essential for all logistics systems
  • Use a password manager: Store complex passwords securely
  • Create passphrases: Example: "Cargo#2026!Ships@Global!"
  • Update regularly: Change every 90 days
  • Avoid logistics terms: Don't use "freight", "cargo", "shipment"
